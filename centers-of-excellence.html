Centers of Excellence: Innovate, Collaborate, Educate

Emerging Tech established 7 Centers of Excellence to pioneer standard-driven approaches that are reliable, efficient, and easily replicated across all industries.

Why we built them

Standard-Driven Approaches, Replicated Across Industries

Emerging Tech established 7 Centers of Excellence (CoE) to pioneer standard-driven approaches that are reliable, efficient, and easily replicated across all industries. Being a technology focused organization, we established various Center of Excellences (CoE) to:

  • Embed Innovation, Collaboration, and Education (ICE) across ET enterprise
  • Develop niche talent, effective frameworks, and demonstrate success
  • Empower ET family members to build partnership to accelerate technology adoption
  • Share knowledge to increase comprehension and forge ahead with best approach
  • Continue to develop and improve standards and processes
  • Integrate disruptive technology into how we conduct our day-to-day business
  • Enable collaborations between ET, academia, government, and other industry partners to provide new opportunities for training
I.C.E.

Innovate · Collaborate · Educate

Innovate

Keep trying to figure out a better way to solve a given problem. Solve the same problem in multiple ways, between two dots there isn’t only one line, and there are multiple ways to get from A to B.

Collaborate

Continue to partner and connect with likeminded organizations, whether consultants, academics, government organizations, or non-profits. We collaborate with different communities to provide the best solutions, vendor agnostic.

Educate

Continue to learn and train. Learn on our own, keeping up to date with standards and staying ahead of the curve, because having information and having knowledge are not the same thing.
