Our approach

Emerging Tech supports technology modernization and system integration from architecture and design through implementation and sustainment. We focus on connecting systems, enabling data interoperability, and building modern platforms that are resilient, secure, and scalable.

We reduce the likelihood of failure through extensive documentation and recording before, during, and after solution design and implementation, and we utilize Independent Verification and Validation (IV&V) to perform unbiased assessments, ensuring each system is built in accordance with its design specifications.

We also specialize in mobile technology — iOS, Android, and Windows-based devices — and our Mobile Innovation Lab (MIL) provides on-demand services anywhere, anytime, with a 123-security strategy covering device platform security, application protection, and data protection at rest and in transit.
