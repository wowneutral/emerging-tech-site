Temporary Mover, Data Center Equipment

Battle Creek, MITemporaryEntry Level$30/hour

Emerging Tech is hiring temporary laborers to help relocate data center equipment, servers, switches, routers, and storage, as part of a facility move at the VA Medical Center in Battle Creek, Michigan. The work involves unracking, transporting, and re-racking equipment on a pre-coordinated schedule, including some overnight and off-hours shifts.

What You’Ll Do

  • Help disassemble and reassemble server racks and components
  • Move and handle IT hardware, including servers, switches, routers, and network appliances
  • Work under the direction of VA facilities and IT staff
  • Label and protect equipment throughout the move
  • Follow scheduled times and site safety/security protocols closely

What We’Re Looking For

  • Able to lift and maneuver 50–75 lbs
  • Prior experience with IT equipment handling, AV/telecom moves, or server room relocations is a plus
  • Punctual, dependable, and precise about following instructions
  • Available for off-hours and overnight shifts; own transportation required
  • Comfortable working in a professional, security-sensitive environment
