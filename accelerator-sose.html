SoSE

Integration Across Domains

System of Systems Engineering (SoSE) focuses on designing and managing complex networks made up of multiple interdependent components. At Emerging Tech, our SoSE teams provide comprehensive solutions to manage and integrate these complex networks across multiple domains, helping clients navigate challenges to achieve reliable, efficient operations.

The lifecycle

Seven Stages, End to End

Concept Development

ET focuses on the process of formulating and refining ideas or concepts that address specific challenges, needs, or opportunities within the healthcare sector. ET is dedicated to addressing healthcare challenges by researching needs, harnessing new technologies, developing and testing prototypes, and overseeing implementation and improvements.

Requirements Engineering

ET is dedicated to eliciting, analyzing, specifying, and validating the requirements for developing or improving healthcare systems, applications, or processes for our clients. We ensure that these requirements align with client needs and industry standards through a structured requirements engineering approach, enabling effective and tailored solutions.

System Architecture

ET assesses the design and structure of the overall information technology (IT) infrastructure that supports the various components of healthcare delivery, management, and administration. We develop and optimize system architecture so components actually connect, scale, and stay aligned with both current and future healthcare needs.

System Integration

ET is dedicated to the process of connecting and coordinating different healthcare information systems and technologies to work together, ensuring data interoperability and a unified user experience across all platforms. We work closely with clients to map out their existing IT landscape, identify integration points, and address potential compatibility issues, ensuring that all systems, such as electronic health records (EHRs), function cohesively.

Test & Evaluation

ET assesses and validates various aspects of healthcare technologies, systems, processes, or interventions. We conduct rigorous Test & Evaluation (T&E) to ensure these solutions meet performance, reliability, and safety standards, providing actionable insights and recommendations for optimization before full-scale deployment.

Transition, Operation & Maintenance

During the transition phase, ET focuses on the adoption and implementation of a healthcare system. The operation phase encompasses the day-to-day use of the system for healthcare tasks, and the maintenance phase involves ongoing support, updates, and optimization from ET to ensure the system’s continued effectiveness and compliance with healthcare standards.

New Capability Integration

ET assists clients by incorporating new technologies, methodologies, or approaches into their existing healthcare systems, thereby enhancing overall capabilities and improving operational efficiency.
