HR Performance Specialist / Admin Assistant

Gainesville, FLFull TimeMid Level$50k–$55k

We’re looking for an operational backbone for our headquarters office: an Administrative Assistant / HR & Compliance Specialist who is detail-oriented, compliance-minded, and comfortable quality-checking others’ work. The role blends HR and performance management with light bookkeeping, payroll support, and general office coordination — equally at home reconciling an invoice or reviewing a colleague’s report before it goes out the door.

What you’ll do

  • Own the company-wide employee evaluation framework, including KPI tracking
  • Run structured employee check-ins and report trends to leadership
  • Support payroll coordination, HR compliance documentation, and bookkeeping
  • Keep audit-ready files, reports, and internal documentation organized
  • Support program managers with weekly status tracking and timecard oversight

What we’re looking for

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Communications, or related field
  • 2+ years in HR, performance management, operations, or analytics-focused roles
  • Strong Excel/Word/Outlook skills and high attention to detail
  • Located within 30 miles of our Alachua, FL office; must be comfortable with a background check and drug screening
  • Willing to be sponsored for a government security clearance, if required

Salary range: $50,000–$55,000.
