Value-Added Reseller Services

Emerging Tech provides Value-Added Reseller (VAR) capabilities to help clients procure, deploy, and manage trusted technology solutions that support secure, scalable, and mission-ready operations.

Trusted technology solutions

Simplified Procurement, Aligned to Your Mission

We support public sector and enterprise needs by providing access to leading cloud, device, licensing, and security platforms, combined with responsive service and implementation support.

Emerging Tech helps organizations acquire and implement leading commercial technologies that support modernization, cybersecurity, collaboration, mobility, and cloud adoption. Our VAR capabilities are designed to simplify procurement and align the right solutions to each client’s operational and mission requirements.
