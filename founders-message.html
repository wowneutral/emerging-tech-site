Welcome to Emerging Tech

A note from our Founder and CEO.

At Emerging Tech, our mission is rooted in service to our nation, our veterans, and the broader public who rely on effective and secure government services. As Founder, I am proud to lead a team that brings deep technical expertise and a commitment to mission delivery shaped by military and federal experience.

Drawing from my background in the United States Air Force and as a Veteran, I operate with a focus on accountability, operational discipline, and outcomes. These principles guide how we support our clients and teaming partners, ensuring reliability, precision, and a consistent focus on those we ultimately serve.

Emerging Tech delivers capabilities across healthcare modernization, cybersecurity, technology and system integration, and mission support services. Our team of over 85 professionals combines technical depth with hands-on experience supporting complex federal environments, including programs that directly impact veteran care and citizen services. Our work with agencies such as the Department of Veterans Affairs and the Department of Defense reflects our commitment to improving outcomes for Veterans while advancing broader public sector missions.

We focus on initiatives that improve healthcare delivery, strengthen system resilience, and enhance operational performance. Our values guide how we execute. We innovate to deliver practical, forward-looking solutions. We integrate across systems, stakeholders, and mission needs to ensure alignment. We optimize performance by driving efficiency, quality, and measurable results.

We believe strong partnerships are essential to mission success. Whether supporting VA medical centers, federal program offices, state government CIO organizations, or industry partners, we emphasize transparency, responsiveness, and accountability. Our Our CMMI Level 3, CMMC Level 1, ISO 9001, ISO 20000-1, and ISO 27001 processes reinforce disciplined execution, and our focus stays on improving services, security, and outcomes.

We recognize that our work ultimately supports people, service members, Veterans, federal employees, and the public. That responsibility shapes our standards and reinforces our commitment to delivering results that matter. I am grateful to our clients and partners for their continued trust and look forward to the opportunity to support your mission.

Gaurav Seth Founder & CEO · U.S. Air Force Veteran
