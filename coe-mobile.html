Mobile CoE: Military-Grade, Anywhere

For over a decade, ET’s mobile experts have configured, secured, and integrated mobile operating systems across every device type.

Mobile Center of Excellence

Just-in-Time Information, Wherever the Mission Is

Mobile devices have become a necessity for both personal and business use. Mobile devices provide access to just-in-time (JIT) information to enhance your mission no matter where you are and what time of the day it is. For over a decade, ET’s mobile experts have configured, secured, and integrated Mobile Operating Systems (MOS) such as Apple iOS, Google Android, and Microsoft Windows regardless of type of mobile devices (phones, tablets, 2-in-1 etc.).

Our past performance includes establishment of enterprise mobile strategy, mobile policies, end-user agreements, end-user training, secure configurations to protect organizations and personal data, integration of device management and application management tools, and much more. We have deployed mobile solutions in healthcare industries, the federal government’s classified work environment, corporate enterprise, and within the academia sector, and are continuing to integrate mobility across America.

We have and are ready to face any of your organization’s use cases, to include but not limited to Bring Your Own Device (BYOD), Corporate Owned Personally Enabled (COPE), Choose Your Own Device (CYOD), Corporate Owned Business Only (COBO), and any other combinations. ET’s Mobile Center of Excellence (MCoE) inaugurated a mobile-first strategy by launching the state-of-the-art Mobile Innovation Laboratory (MIL), where we bring military-grade secure technology to our existing and new clients.

Mobile Innovation Lab (MIL)

On-Demand Services, Anywhere and Anytime

ET’s Mobile Innovation Lab (MIL) provides on-demand services to our clients anywhere and anytime. Mobile device manufacturers are launching new devices every 12 months, mobile operating systems receive updates as often as every 90 days, network providers (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile) push firmware updates as often as every 60 days, and native apps and other commercial mobile apps can receive updates as often as every 30 days or sooner. We are working tirelessly day-in and day-out to continue to develop military-grade secure configuration to protect sensitive data against a periodically changing mobile environment.

More than 80% of the world’s workforce is mobile, frontline workers whose jobs don’t require a desk, computer, or email. Whether you are a small business, large business, or government, mobile-first digital communication platforms are becoming mainstream. We want you to focus on running your day-to-day operations and let us mobilize your workforce. Our goal is to create cost-effective mobile solutions and allow you to adopt a mobile-first strategy.

Our 123-security strategy starts with (1) mobile device platform security, (2) mobile application protection, and (3) mobile data protection at rest and in transit. We have hands-on knowledge implementing Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) tools.
