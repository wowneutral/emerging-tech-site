Healthcare — connected, secure, modern.

Emerging Tech supports the Department of Veterans Affairs and other healthcare organizations with deep expertise in electronic health records and healthcare system modernization.

The mission environment

How we serve

We deliver solutions that strengthen clinical workflows, enable secure and standards-based data exchange, and support EHR implementation, integration, and sustainment. Our work enables continuous and secure clinical operations across distributed healthcare environments, improves interoperability between legacy and modern systems, and enhances data visibility for care coordination, reporting, and decision-making — while reducing operational risk during large-scale modernization efforts.

We deliver these capabilities through mission-aligned architecture designed around clinical and administrative workflows, with security embedded across development, integration, testing, deployment, and sustainment. Our team brings enterprise integration expertise across EHR platforms, revenue cycle systems, analytics environments, and cloud infrastructure — supported by full lifecycle delivery from requirements analysis through ATO and operational optimization.

All work is executed within regulated environments aligned to NIST RMF, federal privacy standards, and continuous monitoring requirements.
