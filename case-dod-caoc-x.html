CAOC-X and Secure Trusted Enclave Workstation: Remote SIPRNET Access

Emerging Tech supported U.S. Army and Air Force projects delivering end-to-end IT consultancy, including CSfC registration providing USASOC a secured end user device capable of mobile access.

Client

U.S. Army and Air Force

Supported individual projects for the U.S. Army and Air Force, providing end-to-end IT consultancy services, resulting in the successful deployment of advanced technology solutions that drive increased efficiency, security, and operational effectiveness.

Key accomplishments

What the Work Delivered

  • Developed and facilitated the submission of a Commercial Solutions for Classified package to receive CSfC Registration, providing USASOC with a secured end user device capable of mobile access.
  • Documented and tested the implemented CSfC solution to ensure it meets NIAP/CSfC compliance standards and is tailored to the Langley Air Force Base specific mission requirements, enabling Langley to more effectively operate and exceed warfighter requirements for software applications.
Services provided

How We Delivered It

Risk Identification

Identified risks and provided appropriate remediation recommends for the concepts of operations (CONOPs) and drafted solution architectures.

Site Assessment

Developed a comprehensive site assessment plan for the onsite security evaluation.

Stakeholder Facilitation

Facilitated discussions with external stakeholders on security matters, documented important considerations, and worked alongside project managers to ensure all tasks are tracked and effectively executed.

Vendor Guidance

Provided technical guidance to vendors during the implementation and integration of the solution, leveraging our CSfC expertise to drive their success.

Test Planning

Developed security test plans and individual test cases.

IV&V

Executed Independent Verification and Validation (IV&V) to identify any deviations in the implemented CSfC solution.

Capability Packages

Generated detailed and comprehensive Mobile Access Capability Packages (MA CP), that included Key Management (KM) and Continuous Monitoring (CONMON) matrices.

Accreditation

Collaborated with the Information System Security Manager (ISSM) to obtain RMF and CDS accreditations.

Submission

Submitted complete CSfC package to the NSA CSfC PMO for registration approval.
