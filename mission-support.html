Our approach

Emerging Tech supports mission execution from planning through delivery and sustainment. We focus on establishing strong program governance, improving coordination across stakeholders, and ensuring operational alignment with agency objectives.

Our teams work within the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), reflecting Lean-Agile thinking with scalable DevOps and continuous delivery, and implement Scrum to emphasize collaboration, responsibility, and goal achievement. On the staffing side, we identify the best candidate for every position — providing candidates of the Emerging Tech standard.

We help agencies
  • Plan and execute programs with clear governance, milestones, and performance tracking
  • Coordinate across leadership, program offices, and external stakeholders
  • Improve organizational effectiveness through change management and training
  • Augment teams with cleared, qualified personnel to meet surge and steady-state needs
  • Streamline administrative and operational processes to improve efficiency and accountability
