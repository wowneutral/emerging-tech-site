Equip. Train. Deploy.

Our internship program gives students real tools, real training, and real client work — not busywork. It's a launchpad into federal IT, built for students ready to contribute from day one.

How the program works

A simple three-step path from student to contributor.

01

Equip

We equip you with the tools needed to succeed — access, equipment, and a real seat on the team from day one.

02

Train

We train you on the tools and processes necessary to do the work, with mentorship from experienced professionals.

03

Deploy

We deploy you to real world clients and mission programs — contributing to work that actually matters.
