Benefits

What We Offer

Good work comes from people who are not worrying about their health coverage or their next paycheck. Here is what we cover.

Compensation

Salary and performance-based bonuses that reward your impact, many positions compensate above market value.

Health Coverage

Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance for you and your family, including fully covered yearly eye exams.

Retirement

401(k) with company match through Capital Group American Funds, with investment aid and tax-advantaged growth.

Time to Recharge

Vacation, sick leave, and holidays, because sustained excellence requires rest.

Income Protection

Short and long-term disability insurance with the full premium paid by Emerging Tech, nothing out of pocket.

Life & AD&D

Group life and AD&D insurance equal to 200% of your salary, up to $250,000, fully company paid.
